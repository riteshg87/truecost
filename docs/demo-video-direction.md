# TrueCost Demo Day video direction

**Status:** direction draft. No generated media exists. No Higgsfield credits have been spent.  
**Master duration:** 210 seconds, with a 180–240 second delivery window.  
**Master outputs:** 16:9 and 9:16 versions from the same approved edit.

## Creative idea

Open with a fictional Indian couple at home asking a plain question: a lower rate does not automatically tell them which offer costs less. Keep the scene contemporary and specific: a dining table, two generic printed offer sheets and a phone showing no private data. Use adult fictional characters with no resemblance to real people. Avoid caricature, wedding imagery, festival shorthand, luxury-home signalling or a staged “financial expert” pose.

The opening lasts 15 seconds. It hands directly to the real TrueCost screen. From 0:15 onward, the film is a live screen recording with Hinglish voiceover, readable English captions and synthetic figures. Generated or illustrative footage must never stand in for comparison results, email verification, saving, history or PDF behavior.

## Aspect plans

### 16:9 master

- Capture at 1920×1080 when possible, with the product centered in a clear 16:9 frame.
- Keep the comparison grid, result metrics and report controls inside the central safe area so captions do not cover them.
- Use the couple scene as a short opening card/shot, then cut cleanly to the real app.

### 9:16 version

- Capture or reframe at 1080×1920; do not stretch the 16:9 screen recording.
- Keep one active UI column and the key result row readable. Use a deliberate vertical crop or a separate phone-width capture after testing.
- Place captions above or below the app viewport, never over **Effective APR**, **Monthly EMI**, **Cost per lakh**, **Total cost**, save status or PDF controls.
- Recheck the 320px readability boundary and every tap target in the final crop.

The same spoken track and 210-second timing can serve both outputs, but each crop needs its own readability review. Do not assume one export proves the other.

## Live product sequence

Use the exact runbook fixture: **Guest bank A** and **Guest bank B**, each ₹10,00,000 for 10 years, at 8.50% and 8.90%, with fresh-draft default fees. The real sequence is guest comparison, **Results**, email **Send code** / **Verify**, automatic save-intent return, **Saved to your account.**, **Saved comparisons**, **Saved result**, **Generate PDF** and **Download PDF**. Wait for each actual state. Do not invent a success transition in editing.

## Production constraint

The user declined spending up to 250 Higgsfield credits and set the budget at ₹0 with no existing credit spend. A tool preflight showed 1,154.9 credits in the account, but that balance is not authorization. Seedance 2.5 was quoted at 97.5 credits per format, so generating both aspect versions would spend credits without approval. The free Genjutsu/Viral options have not been verified as a fit for this brief.

The user selected Higgsfield first and Runway only if Higgsfield fails completely. Verify a suitable zero-credit route before generation in either service. The fallback does not authorize spending existing credits, buying credits or changing a subscription. If neither supports the requested scene within that limit, retain the prepared script and report the missing scene; do not silently replace the requested couple with a different presenter or claim a generated asset exists. Continue preparing the real recording independently.
