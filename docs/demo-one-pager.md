# TrueCost

**See the cost behind the home-loan rate.**

For borrowers in India, two similar interest rates can hide different fees, insurance costs and repayment terms. TrueCost brings the estimated APR, EMI and total borrowing cost into one comparison.

## Why we chose this problem

We chose loan comparison from seven team ideas because the decision matters financially and its costs can be calculated and explained. Prepayment and refinancing offer useful follow-on questions.

- **Insurance-document NLP and NRI banking:** greater accuracy, trust or compliance demands for a first release.
- **Food sharing, stray-animal coordination and spatial organisation:** logistics challenges or a weaker fit with our finance direction.
- **PM Workbench:** a different customer and business workflow.

These are the team’s selection reasons; demand still needs validation.

## The first useful experience

**Compare two offers as a guest.** See the result before creating an account. Sign in by email to save the comparison, revisit it and download a PDF.

The local development flow has passed browser checks with synthetic inputs. The final public release is pending. Figures are estimates based on entered terms, not lender quotes or financial advice.

## What we will measure

**Comparison completion · Sign-in after seeing value · Successful saves · Report generation**

Track the path from first comparison to a saved decision. Agree targets and a measurement window before claiming success.
